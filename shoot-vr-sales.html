How It Works

Built on hand tracking, not controllers.

01

Catch

A pass arrives. Your headset's cameras track your fingers and palm in real time — you catch it with your actual hands, no trigger to hold.

02

Rise

Bring the ball up into a shooting motion. The system reads your hand's real position and speed as you gather into the shot.

03

Release

Let go at the right moment, with the right motion. Made or missed is decided by how you actually shot it — not a tap on a flat screen.
